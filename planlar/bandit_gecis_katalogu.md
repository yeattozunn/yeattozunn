# "Bandit" (asenavfx) — transition and effect catalog

Source: 14 contact sheets, 20 fps (one frame = 0.05 s), 0.00–13.30 s. Instagram UI (heart/comment) ignored.
Look: flat 2D anime, almost a duotone (pure red #C00000-ish + near-black #111). Accents are only white glowing eyes, white stars, orange fire and a pink moon. Every shot is a near-still illustration with small motion (2.5D drift, push-in, a loop of a few frames).

Notation: "1f" = one 50 ms frame. "THR" = 1-bit threshold/posterize frame (pure red/black or black/white, no midtones). "NEG" = inverted white-on-black threshold. "DESAT" = grey frame (saturation 0).

## 3. Recurring motifs

- **1-frame filter flashes are the signature.** About 25 single frames in 13 s are threshold, negative or desaturated versions. They nearly always come in this order at cuts: `DESAT (grey) → NEG (white-on-black 1-bit) → THR of next shot → colour`, or `THR → DESAT → bloom`.
- **Birds as carriers:** crows and bats fly across the foreground, out of focus, on the legs, moon, graveyard and tree shots. They are mainly in-shot parallax, not full-frame wipes. A bird often crosses in the last 0.2–0.3 s before a cut, so the cut feels motivated.
- **Split-panel wipes (half black):** horizontal (T5) and vertical (T10, T20). Always 3–4 frames: one half goes black, then the new shot appears in the other half, then full frame.
- **Glowing eyes and star glints:** white eye slits, star lens flares (1.25, 3.05) and blade glints. Eyes "switch on" at beats (2.60, 6.35).
- **Colour discipline:** red + black only. Grey/white appear only during transition frames. Orange appears only in "fire" bursts (8.50–8.75, 12.90).
- **Footage reuse:** the chorus repeats 3.90–5.95 at 10.75–12.80.
- **Darkness reveals:** shots start from near-black (S11, S14, S15) or end in smoke-to-black (S7).

## 4. Motion inside shots

- Slow push-ins on S1, S4, S5, S12. These are 2.5D: subject and foreground separate slightly, and the gun cloud drifts relative to the cowboy in S1.
- Speed accents inside a shot: fast pull-back plus tilt (2.35–2.45), punch-in (3.45, 13.10), fast zoom-out (9.25–9.30).
- Handheld micro shake on the action shots (S3, S6, S11, S15).
- Short loops / limited animation (anime "on twos/threes"): horse, flag, legs.
- No visible slow motion or speed ramps. The pacing comes from cuts and flash frames.

## 5. Rhythm

- 15 unique shots plus 4 repeats in 13.3 s. Including repeats, the average shot is about **0.55 s**. The median shot is 0.40–0.65 s. Shortest are 0.20 s (moon, tree), longest 1.30 s (WANTED poster with a colour burst halfway).
- A visual hit (cut, flash frame or punch) lands about every **0.25–0.35 s**: hard cuts on the beat, plus 1–3 flash frames in between.
- Transitions are very short: 1–4 frames (0.05–0.20 s). The only longer one is the 0.45 s abstract blur bridge (T15) before the second half.

## 6. Recipe — the 9 most characteristic techniques (ranked)

1. **1-frame duotone threshold flash (THR) at cuts.** Render the first 1–2 frames of the incoming shot (or the last frame of the outgoing one) as pure red/black:
   `geq=r='if(gt(lum(X,Y),70),200,0)':g='0':b='0'` (for black/white use `hue=s=0,lutyuv=y='if(gt(val,70),255,0)':u=128:v=128`). Splice with `trim`/`concat`, or apply to a copy and `overlay=enable='between(t,a,a+0.05)'`.
2. **DESAT → NEG flash pair before a cut.** f1 `hue=s=0`. f2 `hue=s=0,negate,lutyuv=y='if(gt(val,100),255,0)':u=128:v=128`. Each exactly 0.05 s, then a hard cut. This is the most recognisable "edit" moment (5.90, 10.40, 12.75).
3. **Hard cuts on every beat**, 0.2–0.8 s shots, plus a few repeats of a chorus block. In ffmpeg this is just `concat` with frame-exact trims at 20/30 fps. Snap cut times to beat times.
4. **Split-panel wipe (half black).** 1f: `drawbox=x=0:y=0:w=iw/2:h=ih:color=black:t=fill` on the outgoing shot. 2f: new shot `crop=iw/2:ih:0:0` overlaid on `color=black` at x=0. Then full frame. Horizontal variant: same with `h=ih/2`.
5. **Bird/crow silhouettes crossing the foreground.** A black bird-flock clip with alpha (or a PNG animated with `overlay=x='-200+2500*(t-t0)':y=...`), `gblur=sigma=6` for an out-of-focus look, placed in the last 0.3 s of a shot. Optionally scale it up so one bird covers about 60 % of the frame on the cut frame, giving an object-wipe.
6. **Fire/colour burst on a held shot.** For 2–3 frames: `colorchannelmixer`/`curves` to orange, plus `eq=brightness=0.2:saturation=1.6` and a screen-blended glow (`split[a][b];[b]gblur=sigma=20[g];[a][g]blend=all_mode=screen`). Then decay with `eq=brightness='0.25*max(0,1-(t-T)*5)'`.
7. **Smoke-to-black exit.** Overlay a dark smoke clip, ramp `hue=s` to 0 over 0.1 s, then `fade=out:d=0.1` (or `xfade=fadeblack:duration=0.15`). Cut to the next shot in full colour.
8. **Zoom punches and fast pull-backs.** Step scale with `scale=iw*1.3:-1,crop=iw/1.3:ih/1.3` on a beat. For a fast pull: `zoompan=z='if(lt(on,3),1.8-0.27*on,1)':d=1:s=WxH:fps=20` over 3 frames, plus `rotate` for tilt. Add micro shake with `crop=x='(iw-ow)/2+6*sin(t*70)':y='(ih-oh)/2+6*cos(t*53)'`.
9. **Glints and flares.** A 4-point star-flare PNG `overlay` + `blend=screen` on eyes, blades and crosses for 1–5 frames, sliding slightly (3.05–3.30). Eye "switch-on": overlay white slits from a beat.
10. (Bridge, optional) **Abstract blur bridge (~0.45 s)** between halves: footage sped up 3× + `gblur=sigma=30` + `avgblur=sizeX=60:sizeY=1`, one half-black frame, one radial zoom frame, one flat grey frame, two near-black frames, then a reveal from black (`fade=in:d=0.1`).

**Recommended cut rhythm:** average shot about 0.5 s (range 0.2–0.8 s, one hero hold of about 1.2 s with an internal colour burst). A hit about every 0.25–0.35 s. Transitions 1–4 frames. Keep the palette to red + black with flash frames in grey/white. Reuse the middle block as a chorus repeat, and end on a new payoff shot with an overexposed flash and a hard end (no fade-out).
